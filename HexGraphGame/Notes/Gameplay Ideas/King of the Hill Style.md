The "king" would be the creator of the game. Idea being that the wizard created a partially constructed world with their tiles/pieces. The "king" should have a large advantage that comes from this initial setup since it would be them vs all others that challenge them. The challengers would be technically a team, but each of them would be seeking their own benefit since the goal of the game is to gain power/tiles/pieces from other wizards by out performing them in games of wizard's wine. Yu-gi-oh style where the winner yoinks cards from their opponent

Some form of balancing would be needed depending on the setup. i.e. you cant just bring in 4 billion tiles that you have against a king that only has the center and one more row. These limitations would scale based on the initial construction. This creates a form of hierarchy that could make the learning curve of a complex game like this feel good in-world.

To elaborate, the player character would first encounter a lone wizard with a small simple game set up for a 1v1. This could serve as a tutorial and then as the PC gets more power, they slowly get exposed to new concepts that only exist on stronger pieces that require winning (or partially winning) games
